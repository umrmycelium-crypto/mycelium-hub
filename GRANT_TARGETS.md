# Grant & Funding Targets: Mycelium Mind

## 🎯 Primary Narrative: "AI for Cognitive Wellness & Social Recovery"

### 1. Social Innovation Grants
*   **Focus:** Programs that help marginalized populations (e.g., those struggling with hoarding or poverty) regain stability.
*   **Pitch:** Mycelium Mind as a tool for "Cognitive Rehabilitation," helping users organize their lives to escape systemic poverty.

### 2. AI Ethics & Human-Centric AI Grants
*   **Focus:** Moving away from "Black Box" AI toward "Transparent/Visual" AI.
*   **Pitch:** The Idea-Field as a way to make AI reasoning visible and steerable by the human user, preventing AI-dependency.

### 3. Mental Health Technology Grants
*   **Focus:** Tools for ADHD, OCD, or Hoarding Disorder.
*   **Pitch:** A non-clinical, non-judgmental interface that reduces the shame associated with mental clutter and provides a path toward organization.

### 4. Creative Technology Grants
*   **Focus:** New ways of storytelling, brainstorming, and knowledge management.
*   **Pitch:** The Mycelium Mind as a "Generative Thinking Environment" for the next generation of artists and philosophers.
